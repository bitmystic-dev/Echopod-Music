Echopod Music 1.0.4

Features
- Support Echo Show 5 running LineageOS.
- Add a button that returns from the player to the mini player.
- Show the queue name in the landscape player.
- Show slightly larger album art in the player.

Fixes
- Show Quick picks and other Home sections that were missing.
- Keep saved queues and playback positions.
- Keep the mini player closed after it is closed.
- Fix the order of album songs and radio for long albums.
